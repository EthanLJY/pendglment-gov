Founding of the Government.

The Government was founded on 9 September 2026 by GitHub user EthanLJY. The name of Pendglment comes from a portmanteau of "Pending" and "Parliament", which refers to the nature of the Government.

Structure

The Sovereign nation of Pendglment consists of 3 branches; Parliamentary, Judicial and Executive. The government operates under a Parliamentary system with an elected president with executive powers.

Parliament

The Parliament of Pendglment comsists of 11 seats. A Speaker of Parliament is elected every Session to present Laws to the other Members of Parliament. All members, including the Speaker, may vote to pass a law.

Executive Branch

The President of Pendglment is elected every 2 years by all members of Parliament. The President is expected to sign Bills passed by Parliament to officiate it as a Law. A President may also draft Bills.
A Cabinet consisting of 12 Heads of Department help Run Public Services in Pendglment and are also tasked with the Administration of the Executive Branch.

Judiciary Branch

9 Court Justices are Appointed by the President to maintain Law and Order in the Nation of Pendglment. The Judiciary Branch is tasked with the careful interpretation of the Constitution of Pendglment.
